## Rationale for dual licensing

- **EUPL 1.2** provides an EU‑drafted, legally precise copyleft option that
  is comfortable for European public‑sector and institutional use.  It also
  serves as an explicit compatibility bridge to the GPL ecosystem.
- **CC BY 4.0** provides a globally recognised permissive licence that is
  optimal for academic and industry reuse of documentation, white‑papers, and
  technical specifications.

The dual‑licence structure lets the downstream user choose whichever terms
best fit their use case — copyleft reciprocity or lightweight attribution —
without having to negotiate a separate grant.
